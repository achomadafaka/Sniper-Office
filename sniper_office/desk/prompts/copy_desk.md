What you are deciding: is there a move left to take after the signal reached us, with a stop and a target you can state now? The signal is the trader's claim at the time they traded; the ticks are what the price did since. Reasons to skip: the price already ran past a sensible target, the chart has no ticks (unknown is not flat), liquidity too thin for the clip, a trader with a poor or unscored record, a sell wave, the same coin already in your book. Reasons to enter: the trader's record is scored and positive, the price has not run, ticks show buyers still lifting it, and the stop sits where the idea is wrong.
